Findings and corrections

Medium

Deferred CISD confirmation checked before the current C2 wick breach

On the first C4 candle, the previous final-C3 close could emit a new confirmation before that current candle was found to have breached C2. Pine checks the wick first (835-842). The old entry guard rejected the trade, but its event history was wrong.

Correction: Check closed-current-candle wick breaches before deferred C4/CISD checks. A killed setup cannot emit that deferred confirmation.

Impact: Two 30M/3M cases in each 2025 archive; no affected entry or P&L. The same defect appears twice in the older 2026 archive. Regression expectations explicitly record those removed events instead of overwriting the old reports.

Medium

Initial warm-up HTF could not establish a fixed CISD level

Pine initializes h1/l1 from the first lower-timeframe bar (681-685) and can learn a level during the first higher-timeframe candle. The port required a completed previous HTF even for learning levels. A later C2 could consequently have no level and miss CISD.

Correction: Use the first LTF high/low as the initial level-selection comparison, while still requiring two complete HTFs for C2 detection. Bullish and mirrored bearish fixtures failed before the correction and pass afterward.

Impact: A real cold-start correctness defect, reproduced with hand-built OHLC. No change to either frozen yearly trade ledger or the prior 2026 ledger.

Medium

Missing-candle reset availability was stamped at the post-gap open

The engine consumes closed bars, but a gap reset used the new bar open as availableAt. That advertised the reset earlier than this implementation could have learned it.

Correction: Keep the nominal event time and stamp availability at the accepted post-gap LTF close. Propagate availability through C4 invalidation as well.

Impact: No 2025 effect because both audited inputs have no gaps. Prior 2026 execution already marked an exposed missing path unresolved; its ledgers remain unchanged. Event-availability regression coverage was tightened.

Low

C4 status explanation could claim an earlier confirmation that never occurred

The explanation always said an earlier CISD was retained, including when the C4 flag preceded any confirmation.

Correction: Describe a pre-confirmation flag and a later permitted CISD accurately. Preserve the original unusual rule: a preexisting C4-invalid flag does not itself prevent later CISD confirmation.

Impact: Explanation only. Mirrored tests protect the source rule rather than silently replacing it with a stricter filter.

Rules actually implemented

Bullish C2HTF low < prior HTF low AND HTF close > prior HTF low; strict comparisons.
Bearish C2HTF high > prior HTF high AND HTF close < prior HTF high; strict comparisons.
CISD levelFrozen directional-run level from Pine, with doji handling, the ten-bar search, originating index, and persistent reclaim state.
EntryFirst causally available closed LTF price after confirmation. Early CISD waits for C2 close; final-C3 confirmation waits for the surviving first C4 candle close. No fill at a historical CISD-line price.
StopBull: exact C2 low. Bear: exact C2 high. No buffer or tick rounding. A resting stop fills on a touch; indicator invalidation requires a strict breach.
TargetBull: entry + 2 × (entry − stop). Bear: entry − 2 × (stop − entry). Positive initial risk required.
Invalidation / expiryOriginal C2 wick and C4 rules govern setup state. Explicit invalidation exits a position at the next eligible minute open; setup expiry/replacement alone does not exit an entered position.
Carry / no carryCarry has no timed exit. Daily mode exits at the actual session end: normally 15:30 in 2025; 14:45 on the verified Muhurat session.
Execution orderingNo entry-candle earlier-wick exits. Subsequent minute gaps fill stops at the open, targets at the limit. If both barriers occur within one minute, stop first. Missing paths remain unresolved.
Position selectionOne open position per pair, with only unambiguous signals visible on that pair’s card eligible. Simultaneous/hidden/overlapping signals can be skipped. This portfolio/UI policy is additional to Pine.

Evidence

  • Independent signal comparison: literal Pine level/reclaim series and a per-C2 lifecycle walk, without production imports. All frozen levels, origin times, reclaim flags and event sets match.
  • Independent fill audit: every subsequent minute checked for gaps, stops, targets, invalidation, session exit and final open positions; zero discrepancies.
  • Before-fix signal differences and review metadata and all twelve before/after comparisons. Pre-review source and results are preserved in before/; reviewed source snapshots in code/.
  • Regression coverage includes mirrored startup fixtures, deterministic synthetic paths, C3/C4 boundary ordering, the original pre-confirmation C4-flag quirk, exact-wick 2R math, gaps, special sessions and prefix invariance.

Boundaries of this conclusion

  • The Pine file declares an indicator and contains no strategy.entry order or fill model. Confirming-close entry, exact-wick stop and 2R target are our explicit execution assumptions. The run-derived line itself is not a guaranteed fill price.
  • This is a causal adaptation, not identical TradingView historical visuals. It deliberately avoids current-HTF lookahead, retroactive entries and deletion of previously emitted confirmations. Matching exports from TradingView on the same candles have not been run.
  • The independent reference imports no production engine functions, but is still a second manually written interpretation of the source and the documented timing adaptations. It is not the actual Pine runtime.
  • The study measures gross index-price returns, not options P&L. It has no option premiums, strike/expiry selection, spreads, Greeks or contract execution. A 2R index target does not imply a 2R options return.
  • Public data is not exchange-certified. The two complete 2025 archives disagree on 6,847 minute rows (7.36%). Both feeds were tested; daily OHLC cross-checks cannot certify every minute.
  • Historical entry prices assume immediate feed availability. The delayed Yahoo dashboard cannot be assumed to obtain those fills. Its rolling five-day reconstruction and upstream revisions can change state; deterministic IDs do not persist history across data revisions.
  • The verified 2025 replay uses explicit holidays and special sessions. The live dashboard still uses its documented regular-session configuration and weekday estimate, not an automatically maintained exchange calendar. No exact live-session or TradingView-feed parity is claimed.

Rules used

  1. Theoretical NIFTY spot-index reference trades, not an executable index instrument or options-premium P&L.
  2. Identical frozen one-minute input and test period for all three timeframe combinations. First full session is warm-up only. No parameter tuning.
  3. C2 and CISD use the existing causal TypeScript port. A prefix evaluation at each entry must show the same confirmed signal; hidden/ambiguous signals are skipped.
  4. CantoLab is an indicator, not a trading strategy. CISD confirms on a strict close beyond its run-derived level. We assume a theoretical entry at that confirming close; the previously crossed CISD line is not a retrospective fill price. Early CISD waits for C2 close. Late-known C3/C4 confirmations use the first causally available LTF close, after that candle passes invalidation checks.
  5. One position per combination at a time; overlapping signals are skipped. Results are independent and are not a combined portfolio.
  6. Stop is the exact C2 low for bullish entries or exact C2 high for bearish entries, without a buffer or tick rounding. Target is 2 times initial risk. Each trade contributes its own R without compounding. Non-positive-risk entries are rejected.
  7. The confirming candle cannot trigger an exit before its close entry. Resting stops/targets are checked only on subsequent minute OHLC. If both are touched in one minute, stop is assumed first. Stop gaps fill at the adverse open; targets fill at the target.
  8. A post-entry indicator invalidation exits at the next minute open at or after causal availability, unless a resting bracket fills first. Setup expiry/replacement alone does not close an entered position.
  9. No time-based or end-of-day exit. Positions carry across sessions until SL, 2R target, or an explicit strategy invalidation. Trusted execution minutes run 09:15 to 15:15 IST; a close-price entry exactly at 15:15 is eligible, while later auction/finalization entries and executions are excluded. Such final-close entries first encounter a price path at the next regular-session open. This is an index-data execution assumption, not a claim that options trading ends at 15:15; omitting auction-period execution can miss a threshold crossing and is not proven conservative. Raw auction-period bars remain in indicator history. Resume execution at the next trading-session open, applying gap rules.
  10. Missing execution minutes make exposed trades unresolved; their P&L is not guessed. Because positions carry, an unknown position blocks all later entries for that replay. Only weekends and explicitly verified holidays bridge absent days. A still-live position at the end of available data is open, separately from a missing-data unresolved position; neither receives an artificial exit or realized P&L. No re-entry for the same signal.
  11. Gross baseline excludes fees, spread and slippage. A separate flat 1-index-point round-trip deduction is only an illustrative cost sensitivity, not an estimate of options costs.
  12. This is zero-feed-delay theoretical execution. The delayed Yahoo dashboard cannot obtain these historical confirmation-close fills. Its rolling five-session history also differs from this continuous historical replay.
  13. Small single-month sample with Yahoo data-quality limitations; no claim of future returns, statistical significance, independent exchange-data parity or Pine-runtime certification.
